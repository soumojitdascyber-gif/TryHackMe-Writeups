## 🎯 Objective
Learn the different indicators of phishing attempts by examining actual phishing emails and extracting malicious domains.

---

## 💡 Tasks & Answers

### Task: Defanging Malicious URLs
* **Question:** What root domain does the hyperlink in the example point to? (Defang the URL)

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings
* Analyzed real-world phishing email structures.
* Learned the critical practice of "Defanging" URLs (e.g., changing `.` to `[.]`) to safely handle and share malicious domains without accidental execution.
