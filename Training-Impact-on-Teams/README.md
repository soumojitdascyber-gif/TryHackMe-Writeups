# 🚀 TryHackMe Writeup: Training Impact on Teams

## 📝 Room Overview
* **Room Name:** Training Impact on Teams
* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Category:** General / Management

## 🎯 Objective
Quantify the financial and operational impact of cybersecurity training on organizational efficiency, and evaluate vendor integration capabilities.

---

## 💡 Tasks & Answers

### Task: Cyber Security Training for Large Organisations
* **Question:** What is the most efficient way to ramp up the skills of a junior hire in cyber security?
* **Answer:** `Training`

### Task: Write a Cyber Security Training Investment Proposal
* **Question:** What is the name of the dashboard that TryHackMe offers for companies to create customised training paths?
* **Answer:** `Content Studio`

### Task: Vendor Selection (Financial Metrics)
* **Question:** Situation: A team of 20 employees, costing $50,000 annually each. Assumption: Training increases productivity by 4%. What would be the savings due to the increased productivity?
* **Answer:** `40000`
* **Question:** Assuming that training costs $500 per employee, what is the Return on Investment?
* **Answer:** `400%`

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings
* **Operational Efficiency:** Identified structured, centralized training as the most rapid methodology for establishing a common skill baseline and onboarding junior analysts.
* **Customization & Integration:** Recognized the importance of customizable platforms (like THM Content Studio) for tailoring threat scenarios to specific organizational needs and software landscapes.
* **Financial Justification:** Mastered the ability to translate technical upskilling into hard financial metrics, proving a 400% ROI to justify security budget allocations to executive leadership.
