* **Room Name:** Intro to LAN
* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Category:** Networking Core

## 🎯 Objective
Learn about the foundational technologies, topologies, and designs that power private Local Area Networks (LANs).

---

## 💡 Tasks & Answers

### Task: Network Topologies
* **Question 1:** What topology is cost-efficient to set up?
* **Answer:** `Bus Topology`

* **Question 2:** What topology is expensive to set up and maintain?
* **Answer:** `Star Topology`

* **Question 3:** Complete the interactive lab attached to this task. What is the flag given at the end?
* **Answer:** `THM{TOPOLOGY_FLAWS}`

## 🧠 Key Learnings
* Gained a solid understanding of different LAN topologies (Star, Bus, Ring, etc.).
* Learned the pros and cons of various network setups in terms of cost and reliability.
