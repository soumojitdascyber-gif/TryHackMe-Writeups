# 🛡️ TryHackMe Writeup: Security Awareness

## 📝 Room Overview
* **Room Name:** Security Awareness
* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Category:** General / Security Awareness

## 🎯 Objective
Understand the critical importance of security awareness by analyzing the real-world impact of cyber attacks, profiling threat actors, and reviewing basic account security protocols.

---

## 💡 Tasks & Answers

### Task: The Impact of Being Attacked
* **Question:** How many people were affected by eBay being hacked?
* **Answer:** `145 million`
* **Question:** What data was leaked from Playstation being hacked?
* **Answer:** `names, addresses, e-mail, birth dates`

### Task: Threat Actors
* **Question:** Who would most likely be interested in exploiting a business?
* **Answer:** `Cybercriminals`
* **Question:** Who would most likely be interested in exploiting a personal computer for fun?
* **Answer:** `Thrill-seekers`
* **Question:** Who would most likely be interested in exploiting a website to deliver a message?
* **Answer:** `Hacktivists`

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings
* **Breach Impact Analysis:** Analyzed historical data breaches (eBay, PlayStation) to understand the massive scale of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) exposure and reputational damage.
* **Adversary Profiling:** Categorized threat actors based on their core motivations (financial gain, ideological messaging, or personal amusement) to better anticipate potential attack vectors against different organizational assets.
