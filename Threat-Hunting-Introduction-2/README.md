# 🎯 TryHackMe Writeup: Threat Hunting: Introduction

## 📝 Room Overview
* **Room Name:** Threat Hunting: Introduction
* **Platform:** TryHackMe
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Category:** Defensive Security / Threat Hunting

## 🎯 Objective
Go behind the scenes of Threat Hunting to understand the mindset, process, and goals. Differentiate it from incident response, and learn to map threat behaviors using the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

---

## 💡 Tasks & Answers

### Task: Core Concepts & Threat Intelligence
* **Question:** What do you call the approach to finding cyber security threats where there's an active effort done to look for signs of malicious activity?
* **Answer:** `Threat hunting`
* **Question:** In this task, what are we contrasting threat hunting with?
* **Answer:** `Incident response`
* **Question:** Incident response is innately reactive. What is done first thing when an initial notification or alert is received?
* **Answer:** `Triaged`
* **Question:** Threat hunting is innately proactive. What is it guided by?
* **Answer:** `Threat Intelligence`
* **Question:** Threat Hunting and Incident Response aim to strengthen the organisation's what?
* **Answer:** `Security posture`
* **Question:** What is the most obvious and straightforward example of a Unique Threat Intelligence?
* **Answer:** `Indicators of Compromise`

### Task: Threat Hunting Process & Application
* **Question:** What is the primary goal of Threat Hunting?
* **Answer:** `Minimise a threat actor's dwell time`
* **Question:** Upon profiling threats through our Threat Hunting efforts, what should these profiles be translated to?
* **Answer:** `Detection mechanisms`
* **Question:** What is the live malware repository that we touched upon above?
* **Answer:** `theZoo`
* **Question:** What example of Threat Intelligence blends well with environmental noise?
* **Answer:** `Attack Residues`
* **Question:** What should the organisation be extra vigilant in monitoring for announcements of?
* **Answer:** `Zero-day vulnerabilities`
* **Question:** How is characterisation of the subject done most effectively?
* **Answer:** `Attack Signatures and IOCs`

### Task: MITRE ATT&CK Matrix
* **Question:** Which tactic has the most techniques highlighted?
* **Answer:** `Discovery`
* **Question:** Which technique does the three threats have in common?
* **Answer:** `Exploitation of Remote Services`
* **Question:** What technique does WannaCry and Conficker have in common?
* **Answer:** `Inhibit System Recovery`
* **Question:** What's the score of techniques that Stuxnet and Conficker have in common?
* **Answer:** `6`
